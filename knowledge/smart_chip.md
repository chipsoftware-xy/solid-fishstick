# Smart Chip Tree — Company Knowledge

Founded 2013. Technology and system-integration company for smart
identification, RFID, smart cards, access control, tracking, and
automation. Provides hardware, software, deployment, and
customization. PAN India presence and service; offices in Delhi,
Noida, Bangalore, and Pune/Mumbai region.

## Services
System integration; smart card/RFID solutions; ID card issuance and
printing; access control; RFID-based tracking; building management;
card personalization/encoding; hardware and software integration and
deployment; cloud-connected smart card solutions; full-stack software;
customized technology solutions.

Manufactures: PVC, MIFARE, Aadhar, EPIC, financial cards, and sticky cards.

## Industries served
Corporate/enterprise, education (schools, colleges, universities,
libraries), manufacturing, garment industry, defence, fleet/logistics,
asset management, solar projects, buildings/facilities, government and
institutional organizations.

## Case studies (website-stated only — don't over-specify)
Indian Military solution, Microsoft enterprise solution, university/
campus solution. Also: RFID for schools, libraries, garment houses,
colleges, and solar projects.

## Automation
RFID-based tracking, attendance, access control, and ID issuance;
building-management integration. Solutions may combine RFID readers,
cards/tags, software, kiosks, SMS alerts, CCTV, fire alarms, entrance
barriers, and control panels.

## Solution framing
Distinguish: (1) verified existing offerings, (2) possible customized/
integrated solutions, (3) genuinely out-of-domain requests. Anything
in the RFID/smart-card/ID/access-control/tracking/automation space
that isn't explicitly verified should be framed as a possible
customized solution — never as an existing off-the-shelf product.
Only treat a request as out-of-scope if it's unrelated to this domain.
For anything unverified, offer a specialist rather than inventing
specs, pricing, accuracy, certifications, or timelines.

## Do not claim (without separate verification)
AI/ML products, computer vision, facial recognition, ANPR, object/PPE
detection, people counting, video analytics, AI accuracy figures,
ISO/cybersecurity certifications, government empanelment, customer
deployments beyond the three case studies above, that every sold brand
is company-manufactured, prices, turnover/GST/registration details,
delivery/SLA/warranty commitments, guaranteed compatibility, or that
every solution is cloud-based.
